The resources in this repository have been designed to support my teaching of extension maths to junior (years 9 and 10) and senior (years 12 and 13) classes.

The Junior Maths folder contains Word documents with many of my notes and activities written up.

The Year 12 workbook folder contains a PDF called main.pdf which contains notes and exercises for year 12 students doing Level 3 Calculus who want to prepare themselves for the Scholarship Calculus exam.

The Notes folder contains a PDF called main.pdf which contains further notes and exercises for students who have already completed Level 3 Calculus. It covers a wide range of additional topics that would not ordinarily be covered in the standard Level 3 curriculum.

The Weekly Assignments folder contains another PDF with assignment questions that I hand out as homework each week to my Extension Calculus class. Students are encouraged to think deeply about the problems and take the whole week if necessary. Students present their answers to the group at the end of each week. In many cases, the answers in the back of the main.pdf document are credited to ex-students.

The year 12 worksheets and Year 11 workbook are works in progress, paused pending more decisions on the future of the NZ maths curriculum.
