\label{Sum or difference of cubes answers}
\begin{enumerate}
\begin{spacing}{1.5}
    \item 
    Write as a difference of cubes: 
    
    $\omega ^3 -1 = 0$
    
    Factorise:

    $(\omega - 1)(\omega ^2 + \omega +1) = 0$

    Since the condition stated $\omega \neq 1$, we know $\omega - 1 \neq 0$. Therefore, the other factor, $\omega ^2 + \omega +1$ must equal zero, as required.

    \item 
    
    LHS $=\frac{(\sin(x)+\cos(x))(\sin^2(x)-\sin(x)\cos(x)+\cos^2(x))}{\sin(x)+\cos(x)}=$

    $=\sin^2(x)-\sin(x)\cos(x)+\cos^2(x)$

    $=1-\sin(x)\cos(x)$

    $=1-\frac{1}{2}\sin(2x)$

    \item
    
    $\int \frac{x^3 +(2)^3}{x+2} \,dx$

    $\int \frac{(x+2)(x^2 - 2x +4)}{x+2} \,dx$

    $\int x^2 - 2x +4 \,dx$

    $\frac{x^3}{3}-x^2 + 4x + c$

    \item Find all real solutions to the equation:
    
    $x^6 -7x^3 - 8 = 0$

    Use substitution $u = x^3$

    $u^2 - 7u - 8 = 0$

    $(u-8)(u+1)=0$

    Reverse substitution:

    $(x^3 - 8)(x^3 +1) = 0$

    $(x-2)(x^2 +2x+4)(x+1)(x^2 -x+1)=0$

    Neither $x^2 +2x+4$ nor $x^2 -x+1$ have real roots, therefore the only real solutions to the equation are $x=2, -1$

\end{spacing}
\end{enumerate}
